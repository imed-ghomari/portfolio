Quran Life is an Obsidian plugin for **Quran hifdh and daily review**, complemented with **Anki**. It combines **daily portions**, **visual mindmaps**, and **Anki export** — all autosaved to your vault, with Anki handling reviews and scheduling.

**[View on GitHub](https://github.com/imed-ghomari/quran-life)**
**[Documentation](https://imed-ghomari.github.io/quran-life/)**

> Requires [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) (23.10 or newer, desktop or mobile).

### Highlights:
- **Daily portion** — choose the Quran part and a daily target in minutes; the plugin computes an approximate number of verses per day and full cycle length, with Listening (audio reciter) and Reading modes.
- **Mindmap memorization** — create and edit visual memorization maps per surah or part in a built-in mindmap editor; only surahs with a mindmap are exported.
- **Anki export** — configure part and surah order, then export an `.apkg` file from the Anki Deck view and import it into Anki; re-importing updates cards without losing review progress.
- **Mindmap sharing** — import/export mindmaps, verse groups, and notes as `.zip` files to share with other users.
- **Vault-native storage** — everything lives in the plugin's data folder (`mindmaps`, `docs`, `daily`, `splits`, offline audio), so any file sync keeps data in sync across devices.
